# LEOBOG Hi75C Pro — Lighting console · 1180 × 900
- **Idea** Swiss instrument, not dashboard: one picture (the lit keycap field), one 104px index, and a quiet lattice of rules and micro-captions; selection is black inversion, vermilion means live.
- **Palette** paper `#EFEDE7` · ink `#161513` (type, inversion) · rail `#151412` · secondary `#3F3C34` · annotation `#6A665C` · hairlines `#D5D1C7` / `#E2DED4` · signal `#E5401E` (live/current only) · device stage `#191816`, case `#1F1E1B`, keycap `#24221E` · light `#1AE5FF` (content, never chrome).
- **Type** Helvetica Neue → Helvetica → Arial; micro-caps 6.5–8.5px at +0.18–0.22em, text 9–10.5, values 12, effect name 21, page title 50, index 104; tabular figures, −0.045em display tracking.
- **Grid** 1180×900 = 212 rail + 968 main; 40 margins; 888 content = 37 × 24px modules; stage 888×374; controls 408 / 216 / 216 with 24 gutters; key field 16.25 × 6.5u at 47px/u.
- **Rhythm** 4px base; 24 gutters; 18 band gaps; 24 effect rows; 40 preset rows; 27 action rows; 3px keycap gaps; 8/12px inside blocks.
- **Surfaces** flat fills and 1px hairlines only; no drop shadows, bevels or decorative gradients; radius 0 (2px keycaps, 50% knob); the only light is the board.
- **Active** effect = ink inversion + 4px signal tick; swatch = 1px ink outline at 2px offset; preset = 2px signal edge + “in use”; section = 2px signal bar; sliders and status dots carry the signal.
- **Omissions** window chrome, Keymap/About screens, per-key painting, colour picker, import/restore dialogs, toasts, animation; the light field is captured as one frame.
