**Idea** — A terminal-native control deck, not a costume: monospaced columns, box-drawn section rules, a permanent device rail, and the lit 81-key board as the one big object; density reads as engineering, every control is labelled.
**Palette** — `#0B0E0F` floor · `#101516` panel · `#131819` rail/header · `#242C2D` hairline · `#D8DFDB` primary · `#93A09A` secondary · `#6F7D77` meta · `#E8A23C` warm accent (selection, live values, primary action) · `#5FD3A6` signal (link, live, ACK); `#F2A33C` is the selected LED colour — content, not chrome; the other six swatch hues appear only as palette content.
**Type** — One system monospace stack; core scale 21/16/11/10/9/8px with 9.5px keycap legends and 7–7.5px micro-meta; tabular numerals; uppercase labels at 0.12–0.18em tracking.
**Spacing** — 4px base; 12px between cards, 16px rail padding, 22px page insets, 12–16px card padding, 8–9px inside control clusters; 18px telemetry rows; dotted leaders keep columns true.
**Radii & elevation** — 2px chips/buttons, 3px cards, 5px deck, circular encoder; cards are flat (three surface values + 1px rules, no drop shadows); only keycaps and the encoder carry micro-shadows/glow.
**Active states** — selected effect: 2px amber bar + amber label + 7.5% amber wash; selected swatch: double amber ring; selected preset: amber border + dot; focus: 1px amber outline at 2px; Restore is amber dashed with a `⇧` hold cue, never a third hue.
**Omissions** — Keymap and About are rail entries only; no real WebHID transport, import/restore dialogs, disconnected or error states, per-key editor, undo, responsive scaling or light theme; all motion and hover are CSS-only.
**State** — Center Wave · amber 01/07 · brightness 7/9 · speed 4/9 · preset 02 NIGHT SHIFT; the ring on the board is one frame of the wave.
