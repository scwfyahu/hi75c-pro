# Hi75C Pro — Lighting, spec
**Idea.** A moulded warm-graphite console: the keyboard is the only colourful thing, sunk into a deep well, while chrome is defined by light and two-way shadow rather than strokes.
**Palette.** Canvas #151210 · rail #1B1714 · card #241F1B · well #100D0B · ink #F2E9DF/#B4A79B/#8A7D71 · accent clay #D39B7A (the only accent: selection, pips, slider fill) · accent-hi #EDBB98 · key light #D98E6A.
**Swatches.** Ember #D98E6A (on) · Amber #D8A45E · Citron #C4B36A · Sage #8CA98B · Lagoon #6E9F9E · Cobalt #8194BE · Iris #A48BBA.
**Type.** System UI stack; 25/600 title · 18/600 effect · 14.5/600 colour · 12.5/600 card · 11/10.5 controls · 9.5 meta · 8–9/600 caps (+0.16em); tabular numerals.
**Spacing.** 24–26px page inset · 20px row rhythm · 16–18px card inset · 12px side gap · 8–9px chip gaps; rail 16px sides with a 33px section break.
**Radii.** 26 card · 20 well · 16 board case/preset · 13 nav · 10 control/chip · 7 keycap · 999 swatch/pill/track.
**Elevation.** Raised = 1px light inset + 1px dark inset + 28–44px warm drop; inset = 2px dark top + 14–34px internal haze + 1px light bottom edge; no visible borders.
**Active.** Selected nav/effect/preset lift onto a lit surface (inner top highlight) with warm text + a 4–5px clay dot; swatch gets a 3px outer halo, not an outline; sliders use a clay fill + stone thumb.
**Board.** Real 82-key Hi75C Pro geometry (16.25×6.5u incl. knob) at 32px/u; Slow Wave preview is a diagonal Ember alpha ramp per key with underglow pooling in the well.
**Summary.** The running config repeats in the board footer and the session card; Import/Restore/Rescan stay stacked, secondary icon+label buttons.
**Omissions.** No Keymap/About body, dialogs, toasts, window chrome, scrollbars, charts, external fonts/assets or JS; Lighting frame only.
